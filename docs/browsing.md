# Browse and review photos

## Timeline

The `/timeline` page shows photos by date, newest first.

- Select Day, Month or Year. You can also use Ctrl and the mouse wheel, or pinch.
- Day shows all photos.
- Month shows photos with a combined score of 6.0 or more. It shows at least the
  best 3 photos of each group.
- Year shows the best 6 photos of each month.
- If you scrolled past the newest month, a zoom change keeps the month that you
  see.
- Use the rail at the side to go to a month. While you drag it, it shows the month
  below the pointer. A long history shows fewer year labels.
- The URL keeps the zoom, filters and position.

The timeline has the same people filter as [Search](search.md).

## Albums

- The album list shows the cover, name, photo count, date, average rating and
  sharing.
- You can sort by date, name, size or rating.
- Each album page has Photos, People and Species tabs.
- Select a person, known personality or species to see only those photos. Only one
  filter is active at a time. Remove its chip to clear it.
- For a person that you created, a link opens all photos of that person.

### Cover banner

The album page shows a large cover with the album name and photo count.

- On the first visit, the browser gets the original cover file and keeps it. It
  keeps one file for each album, often some MB each.
- This needs [Originals](sharing.md#originals).
- If the file is not available, you see a blurred thumbnail. The next visit tries
  again. This also occurs if the browser cannot show the stored copy.
- A format that the browser cannot decode (for example HEIC in Chrome) stays
  blurred.
- The owner can select "Reposition". Drag the cover or use the arrow keys. Zoom up
  to 3x, then save.
- All viewers see this position. A new cover starts in the centre.

## Photo grids

- Each photo keeps its shape. Columns have different widths, so that they end on
  one line. A page with few photos can have an uneven end.
- With a mouse, point at a photo to see its caption, date and tags.
- On a phone or tablet, each photo has a caption line. The grid has two columns.
  An icon changes it to one column.
- A page has the number of photos that you select.
- The People page and photo grids have sort icons.

## Daily pick

The home page shows one nature or wildlife photo each day, without people. PixdeX
selects it from your best photos. It does not use the same album on two days in
sequence.

## Photo details

The photo page shows the camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter, ISO and
GPS, if the file has them. "View Original" opens the full file.

## Rate and correct

Use "Your review" on the photo page.

- Give 1 to 5 stars.
- Correct the title, species and tags. You cannot edit the description or caption.
- A new index run does not change your edits.
- Search uses your words. A corrected species no longer matches the model's word.
- "Reset to AI" removes your edits.

The combined score is 70% your stars and 30% the model score, on a scale of 1 to
10. Without stars, it is the model score. It controls "Best rated", album ratings
and the daily pick.
